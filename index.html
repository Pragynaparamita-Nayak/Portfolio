About Me

I am an AI Engineer, Independent Researcher, Writer, and Educator passionate about exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, research, and human potential.

I hold an M.Tech in Information Technology from Odisha University of Technology and Research (OUTR), where I graduated with a CGPA of 9.06 and earned the distinction of being a Silver Medalist and branch topper.

My interests span Artificial Intelligence, intelligent systems, deep research, and emerging technologies that can address real-world challenges. Through my professional experience at Infosys, I have explored the development of AI-powered solutions and multi-agent systems, combining technical knowledge with practical problem-solving.

Beyond technology, I am passionate about writing, lifelong learning, and sharing knowledge. I believe that education should inspire curiosity, research should challenge conventional thinking, and technology should create meaningful impact.

My vision is to contribute to technological innovation, advance knowledge through research, and inspire others through writing and education.

I believe in learning continuously, thinking independently, and creating work that makes a difference.
